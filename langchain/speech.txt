================================================================================
                    SPEECH BY PRIME MINISTER NARENDRA MODI
                  ADDRESS TO THE NATION - INDEPENDENCE DAY
================================================================================

Date: August 15, 2024
Location: Red Fort, New Delhi, India
Event: 78th Independence Day Address

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. INTRODUCTION AND SALUTATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

My dear 140 crore family members,

Today, the world's largest democracy is celebrating the grand festival of 
independence. On this sacred day, from the historic ramparts of the Red Fort, 
I extend my warmest greetings to all citizens of India, both here in the country 
and spread across every corner of the globe. 

This day is an occasion to bow down in reverence to the countless brave souls—the 
freedom fighters, revolutionaries, and martyrs—who sacrificed their youth, 
their comfort, and their lives so that we could breathe the free air of an 
independent India. We owe an eternal debt of gratitude to Mahatma Gandhi, Netaji 
Subhas Chandra Bose, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, Bhagat Singh, Chandrashekhar Azad, 
B.R. Ambedkar, Veer Savarkar, and millions of unnamed heroes of our national movement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. VIKSIT BHARAT @2047: VISION FOR A DEVELOPED NATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

My countrymen, we stand at a critical juncture in our nation's history. We are 
living in the 'Amrit Kaal'—the golden era leading up to 2047, the centenary 
of our nation's independence. Our resolve is clear: to transform India into a 
fully developed nation—'Viksit Bharat'—by the year 2047.

This is not merely a government slogan or political ambition; it is the collective 
resolve of 140 crore Indians. Millions of suggestions have poured in from all 
walks of life—from youth, farmers, women, entrepreneurs, and scholars—outlining 
their vision for Viksit Bharat. Their aspirations are our roadmap.

Key Pillars of Viksit Bharat 2047:
  • Modern and Resilient Infrastructure
  • Technological Leadership & Digital Transformation
  • Economic Empowerment of Youth, Women, Farmers, and Marginalized Sections
  • Energy Independence and Green Transition
  • Self-Reliance in Defense and High-Tech Manufacturing (Atmanirbhar Bharat)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. EMPOWERING YOUTH, INNOVATION AND "DESIGN IN INDIA"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Our biggest superpower is our demographic dividend. India possesses the youngest 
population in the world, with boundless energy, talent, and entrepreneurial spirit. 

Through initiatives like Startup India, Digital India, and the National Education 
Policy, we have fostered an ecosystem where our youth are no longer just job-seekers, 
but job-creators. Today, India is home to the third-largest startup ecosystem 
globally, with young minds innovating across AI, space, biotechnology, fintech, and 
renewable energy.

I call upon our youth and industries today: It is time to expand from 'Make in India' 
to 'Design in India' and 'Create in India'. Products manufactured and designed 
by Indian minds should setting global standards of quality and reliability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. WOMEN-LED DEVELOPMENT (NARI SHAKTI)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

True progress is impossible without the active participation and empowerment of 
our mothers, sisters, and daughters. Today, India is moving beyond woman empowerment 
to 'Women-Led Development'.

From the cockpit of fighter jets to space research at ISRO, from sports platforms 
globally to grass-root self-help groups (SHGs) in remote villages, Indian women are 
leading from the front. Through programs like 'Lakhpati Didi', millions of rural 
women are attaining financial independence and becoming pillar strength for local 
economies.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. SELF-RELIANCE & ECONOMIC RESILIENCE (ATMANIRBHAR BHARAT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

In recent years, the world has experienced global supply chain disruptions, wars, 
and economic uncertainties. Amidst these challenges, India has proven to be a beacon 
of economic stability and growth. 

We have made significant strides in self-reliance:
  1. Defense Manufacturing: India is now exporting defense equipment to over 
     80 nations.
  2. Semiconductors & Tech: Establishing semiconductor fabrication plants locally 
     to reduce import dependency.
  3. Renewable Energy: Achieving ambitious solar, wind, and green hydrogen targets 
     well ahead of scheduled deadlines.
  4. Agriculture: Modernizing agricultural practices, supporting natural farming, 
     and enhancing food processing networks.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. REFORMS, PERFORMANCE, AND TRANSFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Our governance mantra remains firm: "Reform, Perform, and Transform." 

We have dismantled hundreds of obsolete laws, streamlined regulatory frameworks, 
and accelerated administrative processes. Corruption and nepotism are being 
rooted out with zero tolerance. Bureaucracy is being made more accountable, 
transparent, and responsive through technology and digital public infrastructure 
(UPI, Aadhaar, DigiLocker).

Our goal is simple: Ease of Living for every citizen, and Ease of Doing Business 
for every entrepreneur and investor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. CONCLUSION & CALL TO ACTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

My dear citizens, 

The path to 2047 requires dedication, hard work, and national unity ('Ek Bharat, 
Shreshtha Bharat'). Let us commit ourselves to duty. Every small step taken by 
140 crore people becomes a giant leap of 140 crore steps forward for the nation.

Let us march ahead with confidence, courage, and pride in our heritage while 
embracing modern scientific temper and progress.

Victory to Mother India! (Bharat Mata Ki Jai!)
Long Live Freedom! (Vande Mataram!)
Jai Hind!

================================================================================
                                END OF SPEECH
